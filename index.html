Story

How I got here

I started in the real world — where operations break, people improvise, and "process" is mostly hope. That's where I learned to respect execution and hate vague thinking.

Since then, every move has followed the same instinct: find the mess, see the structure hiding inside it, and build something that holds up when reality pushes back.

Ops & Logistics → Engineering Mgmt → Product Thinking → AI Systems

The thread connecting all of it? Systems. Always systems. I'm allergic to chaos without a map, and I can't stop turning vague problems into something structured and testable.

"Truth over comfort. Always."

If money didn't matter, I'd be writing stories that hit people hard enough to change how they see life — and building ideas that mess with identity and perception in a good way.

What I believe

Most people don't need "more motivation." They need a better internal operating system. Fix perception and half the "problems" collapse.

The problem I want to solve

I want people to think for themselves. Less pointless suffering caused by human-made nonsense. More peace, clarity, and appreciation for existence.

How a coworker would describe me

Calm, structured, and borderline obsessed with making the work make sense. Asks annoying questions until the fog clears, then builds the plan and pushes it forward.
